## 4. AEO Strategy (Answer Engine Optimization)

### Why AEO matters for TCE

When a homeowner in Newmarket asks ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Siri "who's a good electrician near me?" or "how much does a panel upgrade cost in Ontario?", AI engines pull answers from structured, authoritative web content. The sites that get cited are the ones with clear, factual, well-structured answers to common questions.

TCE is already well-positioned for AEO because of three things we built into the site:

1. **FAQ schema on every service page** — AI engines love structured Q&A. We have 44 FAQ entries across 11 service pages, all using FAQPage JSON-LD markup.
2. **Conversational, specific content** — The service page copy answers real questions in plain language with real numbers ("A panel upgrade typically runs $2,500–$4,500"). AI engines prefer this over vague marketing copy.
3. **Location-specific area pages with unique content** — Each area page references real neighbourhoods, housing types, and local service patterns. This gives AI engines location-specific facts to pull from.

### AEO optimization checklist

**Already implemented:**
- FAQPage schema on all service pages
- LocalBusiness schema with areaServed, paymentAccepted, openingHours
- Service schema linking services to the provider entity
- Conversational FAQ content that matches natural language queries
- Location-specific content on area pages (not templated filler)
- Clear NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across the site
- Pricing information in service content (gives AI engines concrete data to cite)

**Needs to be added:**

1. **HowTo schema on service pages** — For services with clear processes (panel upgrades, EV charger installation), add HowTo structured data. AI engines frequently cite step-by-step processes.

2. **Review schema with individual reviews** — Currently the schema only supports aggregateRating. Add individual Review markup once real reviews exist. AI engines cite specific reviews.

3. **Speakable markup** — Mark the most "answer-ready" paragraphs (opening sentences of each service page, FAQ answers) with Speakable schema so voice assistants prioritize them.

4. **Entity consistency** — Ensure "Top Choice Electrical" appears identically across GBP, website, citations, and social profiles. AI engines build entity graphs and inconsistency hurts.

5. **"People Also Ask" content gaps** — Research the PAA boxes for each primary keyword and ensure the site answers them. Common ones for electricians:
   - "How much does an electrician charge per hour in Ontario?"
   - "Do I need a permit for electrical work in Ontario?"
   - "What is an ESA inspection?"
   - "How to find a licensed electrician near me?"

### Content format for AEO

AI engines extract answers most reliably from content that follows this pattern:

```
[Question as H2 or H3]
[Direct answer in first sentence — 1-2 sentences, factual]
[Supporting detail — 2-3 sentences with context, numbers, or qualifications]
```

Our FAQ sections already follow this pattern. The service page intros should be tightened to lead with the answer, not the setup.
