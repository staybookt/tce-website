Wave 12: callback form + homepage FAQ with schema

New components:
- CallbackForm: low-friction lead capture on the homepage. Name +
  phone + best-time-to-call buttons + optional message. Submits to
  Formspree with source-tracking metadata, fires GA4 form_submit event.
  Drops between Coverage Promise and the cost estimator strip.
- HomepageFAQ: six common pre-call questions in a details/summary
  accordion. Cost, service area, ESA-licensing, emergency response,
  payment plans, warranty. Sits between Recent Work and the closing
  CTA on the homepage.
- FAQSchema: reusable server component that emits FAQPage JSON-LD
  from a list of q/a pairs.

The homepage now has two distinct lead surfaces: callback request
(short, low-friction) and the full quote form on /contact. FAQ
schema makes the homepage eligible for Google's FAQ rich snippets
in SERP. Targets the last +5 score points to bring TCE to ~70/100.

No Meta Pixel — Tim doesn't have Facebook/Instagram presence yet.
